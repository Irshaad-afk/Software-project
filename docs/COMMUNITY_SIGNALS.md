# Community Signals, Not Utility Complaints

PowerGuard lets signed-in residents share a recent observation about a local
power interruption. Reports are **unverified** until quality-reviewed, and even
reviewed reports are not confirmations by the local utility.

The UI groups observations by approximate area and displays distinct recent
reporters to offer *situational awareness*. Three or more recent reporters can
trigger a possible interruption notice, but sample bias, duplication, and
malicious reporting can distort counts.

The reporter may mark their own observation restored. This changes only that
report, never the outage status of the whole neighborhood.

Moderators remove obvious spam, correct misclassifications and attach helpful
source context; they do **not** repair the grid or promise restoration times.

Safety: avoid collecting house numbers or personal identifiers in reports.
