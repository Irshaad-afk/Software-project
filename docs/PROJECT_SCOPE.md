# In Scope / Out of Scope

## In scope

Live forecast lookups, explainable hazard severity, personal preparedness,
city search, login and sign-up, community-submitted observations, voluntary
restoration reports, and authorized moderation.

## Intentionally out of scope

Remote grid restoration, utility outage prediction probabilities, emergency
dispatch, guaranteed notification delivery, automated scraping of CAPTCHA
protected official shutdown notices, and utility-verified status claims.

## Known operational dependencies

- Network connection to the weather and Supabase APIs.
- Correct Supabase email delivery for public registrations.
- Moderators trained not to convert community reports into official claims.
