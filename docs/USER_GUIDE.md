# User Guide

1. Open https://powerguard-weather-app.vercel.app.
2. Search for your town/city or select an available location.
3. Review hourly forecast signals and the *weather hazard index*.
4. Check off preparations such as charging devices, lighting, water and
   offline study material. Checklist state is stored in this browser.
5. Optionally sign up and confirm your email to share a local observation.
6. Identify the approximate area and describe your own observation only.
7. If your power is restored, mark your own report restored.
8. Consult official provider notices for planned shutdowns, emergencies,
   and definitive grid-status information.

If the API is unavailable, the dashboard may show explicitly labeled sample
weather. Do not cite sample numbers as a live forecast in a viva.
